# Authorship and disclosure for this digital reorganization guide

This field guide was created by Christopher Weinberg during independent practical work with AI-assisted digital organization. It grew from hands-on work across multiple storage sources and photo libraries, informed by related research and software projects.

The guide is an independent educational publication, not official product documentation. The repository is owned by Limnmedia; that context is stated here so the guide does not hide its origin. Private files, code, schemas, names, and business material are intentionally excluded.

The methods are offered as a practical synthesis and ongoing field record. They are not a claim to have invented digital preservation, digital curation, personal information management, or AI-assisted classification.

Read [README.md](README.md) for the project goal and [CASE-STUDY-01.md](CASE-STUDY-01.md) for the anonymized personal context.
