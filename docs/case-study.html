Separating a shared digital history

This case began after a breakup, when separating years of shared photos felt almost impossible.

The photo library held ordinary days, important moments, work, travel, and parts of a relationship that could not be reduced to neat folders. Google Takeout provided useful files, but not a clear, usable organization for the separation.

The goal was not to erase the past or decide whose memories mattered more. It was to help each person recover and keep the parts of the collection that belonged to them while preserving the shared history with care.

How can people use AI to make a difficult digital separation safer, clearer, and more humane?

What this shows

Digital organization is often a history problem, not only a naming problem. A cloud photo library may improve access without being the only preservation copy. A careful, reversible process can make difficult choices clearer without pretending that technology can make them for us.
