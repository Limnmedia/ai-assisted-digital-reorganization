Beginner guide

Start with a small question

Whether you are organizing your own files or helping a grandparent, family member, or friend, begin with one clear question. If the files belong to someone else, keep them involved in the important choices.

The simple plan

  1. Ask what you need. Are you looking for photos, protecting old files, or sorting a shared collection?
  2. Leave the originals alone. Do not delete, move, rename, or upload anything yet.
  3. Make a simple list. Write down the computers, drives, phones, cloud accounts, exports, and backups.
  4. Ask AI for ideas. Show it a small, redacted list. Ask for groups and questions—not changes.
  5. Review and test. Decide together when the files belong to someone else. Try one small, approved copy and check that it worked.

A safe chatbot prompt

Show the chatbot a small, redacted file list and ask:

Help me group this file list and identify questions to ask before I change anything. Do not suggest deleting or changing files.

Never paste passwords, recovery codes, private keys, or a full private archive.

When to stop

Pause if files are missing, the owner is unsure, private information could be exposed, or a change would be hard to undo.
