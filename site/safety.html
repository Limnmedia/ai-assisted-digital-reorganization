Before you begin

Safety comes first

Digital organization can cause privacy loss or destroy the only usable copy. Keep the original files safe and keep a person responsible for important decisions.

Protect originals

Keep an untouched source copy. Work from a copy or staging area. Do not delete while you are still guessing.

Protect private information

Do not paste passwords, recovery codes, private keys, or a full personal archive into a chatbot. Redact names and paths before sharing a file list.

Keep human approval

People approve deletion, merging, overwriting, access changes, bulk renaming, and public sharing. AI suggestions are not permission.

Check the result

Compare counts, open sample files, check dates and metadata, and use checksums when useful. Record failures instead of hiding them.

Stop when unsure. Pause if files are missing, a cloud service behaves unexpectedly, the AI conflicts with what the owner knows, or a decision could affect someone’s rights or privacy.
