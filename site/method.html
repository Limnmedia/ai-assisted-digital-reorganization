One pattern, two levels

The method

The human and technical guides explain the same workflow. One uses everyday language. The other adds formal terms, preservation concepts, and evidence models.

  1. Set the question.Choose a clear goal and boundary.
  2. Discover.Make a read-only inventory.
  3. Preserve history.Record where files came from.
  4. Separate facts from guesses.Keep evidence visible.
  5. Use independent dimensions.Describe source, subject, type, and state separately.
  6. Model confidence.Keep uncertainty instead of forcing an answer.
  7. Use AI for review.Ask for suggestions and questions.
  8. Compare copies.Tell duplicates, edits, exports, and backups apart.
  9. Propose changes.Write down the evidence and the plan.
  10. Stage reversible operations.Copy first and test a small sample.
  11. Verify.Check counts, files, dates, and checksums.
  12. Stop and escalate.Pause when risk or uncertainty is high.
  13. Relate the work to preservation practice.Use the right level of documentation for the collection.
