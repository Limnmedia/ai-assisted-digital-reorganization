Beginner guide

If you are helping someone

You may be helping a grandparent, family member, friend, or client with an old computer, external drive, cloud account, or photo library. Helping does not mean taking ownership of the files. The file owner makes the important choices.

Start with these steps

  1. Ask what they want. Are they looking for photos, protecting old files, or separating a shared collection?
  2. Do not change anything yet. Do not delete, move, rename, or upload files.
  3. Find the sources. Look for the computer, USB drives, external drives, phones, cloud accounts, exports, and backups.
  4. Protect the originals. Make a backup or safe working copy. If possible, keep it on a different drive.
  5. Make a simple list. Start with folder names, file names, sizes, dates, and where each item came from.
  6. Use AI for questions. Show it a small, redacted list. Ask for groups and questions, not changes.
  7. Review together. The owner decides what a file means and what should happen to it.
  8. Test a small copy. Copy one approved group to a new folder, open the files, and check the result.
  9. Stop when unsure. Keep the current state if anything is missing, private, or hard to undo.

A safe chatbot prompt

You can show an online chatbot a redacted file list and ask:

I am helping my grandmother organize files on an old computer. Here is a redacted list of folders and files. Do not tell me to delete anything. Help me group the files, identify questions I should ask her, and suggest one safe first step.

Never paste passwords, recovery codes, private keys, a full archive, or unredacted medical, financial, legal, or personal records.

A small example

OldComputer/
  photos/
    IMG_1042.jpg
    IMG_1042-edited.jpg
  downloads/
    final-final-report.docx
  backup/
    photos-old/

AI might suggest that the two photo files are related and that photos-old may be a backup. The owner may correct that suggestion. If the owner agrees, copy only the approved files to a new folder, open them, and leave the originals unchanged.
