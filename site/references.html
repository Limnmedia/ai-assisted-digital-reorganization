Digital preservation and personal archives

  • Digital Preservation Coalition, Digital Preservation Handbook - a peer-reviewed, practical knowledge base for managing digital resources over time.
  • Digital Preservation Coalition, Getting Started - rapid assessment, asset registers, multiple copies, fixity checks, recovery tests, and process documentation.
  • Library of Congress, Personal Digital Archiving - personal digital archiving resources, including organizing and preserving personal collections.
  • Library of Congress, PREMIS - a preservation-metadata standard covering digital objects, events, agents, and rights; useful background for provenance and change records.
  • National Archives, Preserving Your Digital Memories - public guidance on selecting, organizing, copying, and checking personal digital photographs, audio, video, email, and records.
  • National Archives, Digital Preservation - preservation risks and prioritization guidance for digital files.

AI governance and human oversight

  • NIST, Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework 1.0 - a voluntary framework for managing risks to people, organizations, and society associated with AI.
  • NIST, Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile - a companion profile describing generative-AI risks and suggested risk-management actions. The stable DOI is 10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1.
