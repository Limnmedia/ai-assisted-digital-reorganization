Use it with a chatbot

Give the guide to your chatbot first.

Copy this public link into a chatbot that can read web pages:

https://limnmedia.github.io/ai-assisted-digital-reorganization/

Then copy this prompt:

Please read this public guide before helping me:
https://limnmedia.github.io/ai-assisted-digital-reorganization/

Help me use its safe first steps. Do not ask me to upload a full archive or suggest deleting, moving, renaming, or changing files yet. Start by asking what I want to find or protect, then help me make a read-only inventory. Keep facts separate from guesses and leave final decisions to me.
Remember what AI is good at.

AI is useful for sorting a long list, spotting patterns, explaining unfamiliar terms, and suggesting questions or labels. It is not good at knowing what a memory means to you, proving who owns a file, judging what is safe to delete, or making an important personal decision. Let it help with the work it can see. Keep the decisions that need human knowledge and care with a person.

If the chatbot cannot open links, start with the beginner guide and paste only a small, redacted file list.

How to start

See what you have before you change anything.

AI can help you make sense of a long file list. It cannot know what matters to you or decide what is safe to delete.

  4. 04ReviewLet AI suggest, not decide.

A real reason to begin

Sometimes organizing files is part of moving on.

The case study began after a breakup, when separating years of shared photos felt almost impossible. Google Takeout provided the files, but not a clear way to understand them. The method grew from the need to make a difficult digital separation safer and more humane.
