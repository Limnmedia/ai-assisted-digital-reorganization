# Boundary and independence

This directory is a public-facing educational guide, kept separate from the LIMNINDEX product documentation and from private Weinberg Data ORG project material.

## Three distinct contexts

- **LIMNINDEX** is the LIMNMEDIA-branded software product, with its own codebase, architecture, roadmap, and internal documentation.
- **Weinberg Data ORG 2026** is a real-world organizational project with private source material.
- **This field guide** is an independent research and practice artifact for ordinary people using AI-assisted methods to understand and reorganize accumulated digital material.

Related LIMNMEDIA projects, including LIMNINDEX and LIMNAI, may be referenced as adjacent research or tooling examples. The guide may describe general methods learned from experience with those contexts, but it is not a product manual, project record, implementation specification, or endorsement. It does not require LIMNINDEX, LIMNAI, or any other related project, and LIMNMEDIA has not been represented as endorsing every recommendation here.

## Public-safe abstraction rule

Keep only general methods such as read-first discovery, provenance, confidence, independent classification dimensions, human review, copy-first operations, manifests, and verification. Do not add private code, database or application schemas, internal names, private filenames, business strategy, credentials, unreleased product plans, or identifying case-study details.

When an example comes from real work, generalize or remove people, locations, accounts, filenames, quantities, and business-sensitive material. If a detail is not needed to teach the method, leave it out.
