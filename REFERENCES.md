# References for digital preservation and AI-assisted organization

Links checked 2026-11-07. These authoritative sources support the digital preservation, personal digital archives, and AI-risk portions of the guide. They do not validate every local recommendation or establish that this guide is first. Read [METHOD.md](METHOD.md) for the plain-language workflow and [AI-ASSISTED-METHOD.md](AI-ASSISTED-METHOD.md) for the technical version.

## Digital preservation and personal archives

- Digital Preservation Coalition, [Digital Preservation Handbook](https://www.dpconline.org/handbook/) - a peer-reviewed, practical knowledge base for managing digital resources over time.
- Digital Preservation Coalition, [Getting Started](https://www.dpconline.org/handbook/getting-started) - rapid assessment, asset registers, multiple copies, fixity checks, recovery tests, and process documentation.
- Library of Congress, [Preservation](https://www.loc.gov/preservation/) - preservation education and digital preservation resources from the Library of Congress.
- Library of Congress, [PREMIS](https://www.loc.gov/standards/premis/) - a preservation-metadata standard covering digital objects, events, agents, and rights; useful background for provenance and change records.
- National Archives, [Preserving Your Digital Memories](https://www.archives.gov/files/preservation/formats/pdf/preserving-your-digital-memories.pdf) - public guidance on selecting, organizing, copying, and checking personal digital photographs, audio, video, email, and records.
- National Archives, [Digital Preservation](https://www.archives.gov/preservation/digital-preservation) - preservation risks and prioritization guidance for digital files.

## AI governance and human oversight

- NIST, [Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework 1.0](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) - a voluntary framework for managing risks to people, organizations, and society associated with AI.
- NIST, [Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence) - a companion profile describing generative-AI risks and suggested risk-management actions. The stable DOI is [10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1).

## Reading and citation policy

For a factual claim, prefer a stable primary or authoritative source and record the date checked. Cite the exact source that supports the claim. Mark local observations, recommendations, and open questions so readers do not mistake them for externally established facts.
